# PTT features JJptt doesn't do yet

[中文](missing-features.md) ｜ [日本語](missing-features.ja.md)

In short:

- **Chrome extension**: PTT's own screen (the black terminal screen) is all there, so **anything PTT can do, you can do in the extension**, with PTT's usual keys. JJptt's reading mode and FACT sit on top of it.
- **Android app**: the app is a new screen made for phones, not PTT's own screen, so it does the everyday things only. Below is what the app **can't** do yet, and what to do instead.

## What the Android app can do

So you don't miss what's already there:

- Log in, and log in automatically
- Open your favourites (folders included), the popular boards, or any board by typing its name
- Browse a board's articles; search by title, author or push count
- Read articles, pictures and pushes, with FACT's tags
- Push (推, 噓, →)
- Reply (with or without quoting the article)
- Post (with a category)

## What the Android app can't do yet

| You want to | The app today | What to do instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Read or send mail** (PTT's mailbox) | No mailbox | On a computer, use the Chrome extension: press `M` on the main menu |
| **Reply to the author by mail** (privately, not on the board) | Replies go to the board only | In the extension: press `y` on the article and choose the author's mailbox |
| **Edit your own article** | No | In the extension: select the article in the list and press `E` |
| **Delete your own article** | No | In the extension: select the article in the list and press `d` |
| **Cross-post** an article to another board | No | In the extension: select the article in the list and press `Ctrl` + `X` |
| **Read a board's archive** (精華區) | No | In the extension: press `z` in the board. To just read it, most boards' archives are also on PTT's web site (www.ptt.cc) |
| **Browse boards by category** | Favourites, popular boards, or typing a board's name | If you know the name, type it on the app's home screen. To browse, use 分類看板 on the extension's main menu |
| **Read hidden or private boards** | Not possible. The app reads articles from PTT's web site, which only has public boards | Use the extension |
| **Change your PTT favourites** | The ☆ at the top right of a board adds it to the app's favourites and removes it again. That stays on the phone: it doesn't change the favourites in your PTT account, and there's no reordering | In the extension's board list, press `a` to add and `d` to remove. The next time the app opens and logs in, it brings in the boards you added (ones you removed in the extension, remove in the app with ☆) |
| **Chat, send 水球 (instant messages), see your friends list** | No | Use the extension |
| **Look up someone's profile** (名片, logins) | No | In the extension: main menu → Talk → Query, then type the account |
| **Change your profile**: nickname, signature, password | No. The app posts and replies without a signature | In the extension: main menu → User |
| **Vote** | No | In the extension: press `V` in the board (forgot the key? press `h` for help) |
| **Moderator work**: board settings, pinned posts, deleting others' posts, bans | No | Use the extension |

## Using PTT's own features in the extension

- In the extension, every screen outside reading mode is PTT's own screen. In reading mode, `Esc` takes you back to it.
- The keys are the same as in any other PTT program; the keys in the table are PTT's own. Press `h` on any screen for help.
- Not installed yet? [How to install the Chrome extension](desktop.en.md).
